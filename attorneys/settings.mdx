# Attorney Settings

The Settings page is where you manage all aspects of your ExpertMD account. Access it by clicking **Settings** in the left sidebar.

## Settings tabs

### Profile

Update your personal and professional information:

- Name, email, and phone number
- Law firm name and address
- Practice areas and licensed states
- Bar credentials

See [Profile Completion](/getting-started/profile-completion) for details on each field.

### Billing

Manage your subscription tier and payment methods:

- View your current subscription plan
- Upgrade or downgrade your tier
- Update payment method for subscription fees
- View billing history

### Intake

Configure your client intake form:

- Set or change your URL slug
- Enable or disable your intake form
- Preview your intake form

See [Client Intake](/attorneys/client-intake) for full details.

### Encryption

Manage your end-to-end encryption passphrase:

- Change your passphrase
- View your encryption status (locked/unlocked)
- Lock your vault manually

See [Encryption Setup](/getting-started/encryption-setup) for full details.

### Team

Manage team members and permissions:

- Invite new team members
- View current team members
- Remove team members

See [Team Collaboration](/attorneys/team-collaboration) for full details.

### Notifications

Configure which notifications you receive:

- In-app notification preferences
- Email notification preferences
- Notification frequency settings

## Account management

At the bottom of the Settings page, you can find account management options:

- **Sign out** -- Sign out of your current session
- **Delete account** -- Permanently delete your account and all associated data

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your cases, invoices, documents, and team connections will be permanently removed. Export any data you need before deleting your account.
</Warning>
