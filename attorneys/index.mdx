## Typical workflow

Here is the typical workflow for an attorney on ExpertMD:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a medical expert">
    Browse the platform or receive a referral link from a medical expert. Submit an intake request through their form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expert reviews your request">
    The doctor reviews your intake request and accepts or declines. If accepted, a case is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collaborate on the case">
    Upload documents, schedule appointments, add collaborators, and communicate through the case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive and pay invoices">
    The doctor sends you invoices for their services. Review the itemized breakdown and pay online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the engagement">
    When the case is resolved, the doctor marks it complete. All documents and records remain accessible.
  </Step>
</Steps>
