# Referral Program

ExpertMD rewards you for helping grow the platform. When you refer a new doctor or attorney who signs up and starts using ExpertMD, you earn **25% of the platform fees** generated by that user for **12 months** from their sign-up date.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your referral link">
    Find your unique referral link on your dashboard or in **Settings > Referrals**. Copy it with one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your link">
    Share the link with colleagues, doctors, or other attorneys via email, social media, or in person.

    <Frame caption="Referral card on the attorney dashboard">
      <img src="/images/attorneys/referral-dashboard-card.png" alt="Referral card on the attorney dashboard" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="They sign up and use ExpertMD">
    When someone creates an account through your link and starts transacting on the platform, you earn commissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn for 12 months">
    For every invoice paid on the platform involving your referral, you earn 25% of the 2% platform fee. This continues for 12 months from their sign-up date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Earnings example

If you refer a doctor who generates $200,000 in invoices over 12 months:

- Platform fee collected: $200,000 x 2% = $4,000
- Your referral commission: $4,000 x 25% = **$1,000**

<Info>
  There is no limit to the number of people you can refer. Each referral earns independently for 12 months.
</Info>

## Tracking and payouts

Track your referrals and commissions in **Settings > Referrals**. Commissions are paid out through your Stripe Connect account if you are a doctor, or via check/ACH if you are an attorney. Contact support for payout setup.
