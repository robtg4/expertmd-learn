## General questions

### What is ExpertMD?

ExpertMD is a platform that connects attorneys with medical expert witnesses. It provides tools for intake management, case collaboration, invoicing, and secure document sharing, all protected by end-to-end encryption.

### Who can use ExpertMD?

ExpertMD is available to:

- **Medical professionals** -- Physicians, surgeons, and other medical experts who provide expert witness services
- **Attorneys** -- Legal professionals who need qualified medical expert witnesses for their cases

### How much does ExpertMD cost?

- **For doctors** -- ExpertMD charges a 2% platform fee on each invoice payment. There are no subscription fees.
- **For attorneys** -- Creating an account and submitting intake requests is free. Subscription tiers are available for team collaboration features.

### Is my data secure?

Yes. ExpertMD uses end-to-end encryption to protect sensitive case data. Your passphrase never leaves your device, and ExpertMD staff cannot read your encrypted data. See the [Security](/security) section for details.

### How do I contact support?

Email us at support@expertmd.io. We typically respond within 1 business day.

### Can I use ExpertMD on mobile?

ExpertMD is a web application that is responsive and works on mobile browsers. There is currently no native mobile app, but the web experience is fully functional on phones and tablets.
