# What's New

Stay up to date with the latest changes to ExpertMD. We ship improvements regularly to make your experience better.

---

## April 2026

### Referral Program Launch

_April 18, 2026_

We are excited to launch the ExpertMD Referral Program. Share your unique referral link with colleagues and earn **25% of the platform fees** generated by each person you refer, for **12 months** from their sign-up date.

- Available to both doctors and attorneys
- No limit on the number of referrals
- Track your referrals and earnings from your dashboard

[Learn more about the Referral Program](/doctors/referral-program)

---

### Payment Reminders

_April 15, 2026_

Doctors can now send manual payment reminders for outstanding invoices. A reminder email is sent to the attorney with the invoice summary and a direct payment link.

- Available for invoices in **Sent** or **Overdue** status
- **1-hour cooldown** between reminders to prevent spam
- Reminders CC the doctor for record-keeping

---

### Itemized Invoice Line Items

_April 15, 2026_

Invoices now display **itemized line items** on the Stripe payment page, in emails, and on PDF downloads. Each service appears as its own line item with quantity, rate, and amount.

Previously, invoices showed a single lump-sum amount. Now attorneys can see exactly what they are paying for:

- Individual service descriptions (e.g., "Medical Record Review," "Deposition Preparation")
- Per-service quantity and rate
- Separate tax line item
- Small rounding adjustment if needed for exact Stripe total matching

---

### PDF Inline Display

_April 15, 2026_

Invoice PDFs now open **inline in the browser** when accessed from email links, instead of triggering a download. This eliminates the issue where some browsers showed a blank page (`about:blank`) when trying to open the PDF.

- PDFs display directly in your browser tab
- Save or print from your browser's built-in controls
- **90-day download tokens** with user-friendly error pages for expired links

---

### Client Intake for Attorneys

_April 10, 2026_

Attorneys can now set up their own client intake forms with custom URL slugs. Share your intake URL with clients so they can submit case information directly to your ExpertMD dashboard.

- Custom URL slug (e.g., `expertmd.io/client-intake/smith-law`)
- Collect client contact info, case type, description, and documents
- Notifications when new submissions arrive

[Learn more about Client Intake](/attorneys/client-intake)

## March 2026

### End-to-End Encryption

_March 2026_

We shipped end-to-end encryption for sensitive case data. Your passphrase protects your private key, and per-case symmetric keys encrypt case notes, documents, and other sensitive content.

- PBKDF2 key derivation with unique salts
- Asymmetric keypair per user (public key stored, private key encrypted)
- Per-case AES-256 symmetric keys
- Lock/unlock mechanism for browser sessions

[Learn more about Encryption](/security/encryption-overview)

---

### Stripe Connect Integration

_March 2026_

Doctors can now connect their Stripe accounts to receive payouts directly to their bank accounts. The 2% platform fee is automatically deducted from each invoice payment.

- One-time Stripe onboarding flow
- Automatic payout scheduling
- Full earnings and payout history in the Billing section

[Learn more about Stripe Connect](/doctors/stripe-connect)
