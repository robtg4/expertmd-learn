# Welcome to the ExpertMD Learning Center

ExpertMD is the modern platform that connects **attorneys** with **medical expert witnesses**. Whether you are a physician looking to offer your expertise or an attorney seeking qualified medical professionals for your cases, ExpertMD streamlines the entire workflow from initial intake through invoicing and payment.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email or Google account. ExpertMD uses Clerk for secure authentication with multi-factor support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Fill in your professional details, specialty information, and billing address. Doctors set up their intake form; attorneys configure their client intake.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up encryption">
    Create a passphrase to enable end-to-end encryption. This protects sensitive case data so that only authorized parties can access it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    Doctors receive and review intake requests from attorneys. Attorneys find and engage medical experts for their cases. Cases, documents, appointments, and invoices are all managed within the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

- **Secure intake pipeline** -- Customizable intake forms with vanity URLs for easy sharing
- **End-to-end encryption** -- Sensitive case data is encrypted with your personal passphrase
- **Integrated invoicing** -- Create itemized invoices with tax, send them via email, and track payment status
- **Stripe-powered payments** -- Secure payment processing with Stripe Connect for doctor payouts
- **Team collaboration** -- Invite team members and collaborate on cases together
- **Referral program** -- Earn 25% of platform fees for 12 months when you refer new users
